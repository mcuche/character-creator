# Impeccable Design Context — Dragonbane Character Creator

## Design Context

### Users
Tabletop RPG players using this for **Dragonbane** (Free League Publishing). Two contexts:
- **Solo pre-session prep**: Laptop at home, no time pressure, wants to explore the system.
- **Session zero at the table**: Mix of phones and laptops, GM present, people making decisions quickly.

Both contexts matter. The interface must be comfortable for slow careful reading AND fast scanning under social pressure.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **stark, mythic, grounded**

Dragonbane is Scandinavian dark fantasy — bleak wilderness, harsh survival, ancient myth. Not Tolkien-beautiful. Not grimdark-edgy. More like: a wolf-skin in a firelit hall, iron tools, old runes on cold stone.

Emotional goal: the interface should feel like it *belongs to the world* — purposeful, a little severe, absolutely trustworthy. Players should feel ready for adventure, not like they filled out a form.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark. Always dark. The game is played at night, at tables, under dim light.
- **Palette**: Stone/charcoal base with amber-gold accents (already established in code). Formalize with OKLCH. Accents are rare and meaningful — not decorative.
- **Typography**: Cinzel for headings (already in use, classical weight). Body text needs a warm serif — **Alegreya** or **Vollkorn** — something bookish and slightly heavy-set. NOT Crimson Pro (too delicate), not system-ui (no personality).
- **Visual texture**: Subtle — grain, noise, or micro-pattern to prevent flat-dark looking like a generic SaaS dark mode. NOT parchment. NOT leather. Think: iron, stone, ink on vellum.
- **Decoration**: Sparse but intentional. Rune-like SVG dividers, geometric borders, typographic ornaments — not emoji, not icon libraries.

### Anti-References
- **Heavy parchment / skeuomorphic**: No aged paper, fake leather, crumpled textures.
- D&D Beyond: Too polished and corporate.
- Generic SaaS dark mode: Flat gray-on-dark with cyan accents — could be any app.

### Design Principles
1. **Atmosphere through restraint** — evocativeness comes from careful typography and color, not decoration. Let the content carry the world.
2. **Table-ready legibility** — everything must read at arm's length on a dim screen. Generous tap targets, strong contrast, no tiny affordances.
3. **The app belongs to the world** — every design decision should feel like it could exist in the Dragonbane rulebook. If it looks like a web app, it's wrong.
4. **Earn the accent** — gold/amber is precious. Use it for active states, key actions, and hierarchy — not as decoration.
5. **Dark is a feature, not a default** — dark mode was chosen, not defaulted to. Lean into it. Rich blacks, subtle depth, ink-and-shadow contrast.
