## Core Mechanic
Roll **D20 ≤ skill level** = success. **1 = Dragon** (critical). **20 = Demon** (critical fail; cannot push).
**Boon:** roll 2D20, take lowest. **Bane:** roll 2D20, take highest. Extra boons/banes add one D20 each; boons cancel banes.
One attempt per action outside combat. Failure always leaves a way forward — at a cost.

**Pushing:** After a fail, re-roll and immediately gain a condition (justify it; can't pick one you have). Can't push a Demon. PCs only.

**Opposed:** Both roll. You fail if your roll fails regardless. Both succeed → lower result wins. Only active party can push.

**Attribute rolls:** Used when no skill applies (e.g. STR to lift). If a skill exists, use it instead.

**Helping:** One other PC or NPC can give you a boon on a roll (declared before rolling; must be physically present and capable). In combat, helping costs the helper's action.

## Encumbrance
Carry up to **STR/2 (rounded up)** items in your Inventory. The following do **not** count toward encumbrance: weapons at hand (up to 3; recorded in Weapons section); shields (count as weapons); worn armor and helmet; tiny items; <100 coins (100–199 = 1 item, 200–299 = 2, etc.); up to 4 rations count as 1 item.

**Heavy items** have weight 2+ and take that many Inventory rows. **Tiny items** (fit in a closed fist) take no space. **Backpack:** +2 carrying capacity (one at a time; doesn't count as an item).

**Over-encumbered:** Can carry more temporarily, but must make a STR roll to move in a combat round or walk a shift. Fail = drop gear or stay put. Carrying another person = automatically over-encumbered; cannot fight.

## Combat

### Initiative & Actions
Rounds ≈ 10 seconds. Everyone draws a random **initiative card (1–10)**; lower acts first. Turn = **one action + movement** (split freely). **Waiting:** Swap your card with any unspent combatant (they can't refuse). **Surprise:** Attackers choose any card; victims draw from the rest. **Ambush:** Victims roll AWARENESS (bane if attackers are prepared); failures get bottom cards. Spent turns flip card face down.

**Actions:** Dash (×2 move) · Melee Attack · Ranged Attack · Parry *(reaction)* · Dodge *(reaction)* · Pick Up Item · Equip/Unequip Armor · First Aid (HEALING at 0 HP) · Rally (PERSUASION on downed ally) · Break Down Door · Pick Lock (SLEIGHT OF HAND; bane without lockpicks) · Use Item · Activate Ability · Cast Spell · Help (gives ally a boon; costs your action) · Round Rest (D6 WP; once per shift)

**Free actions (once each, own turn):** Draw/sheathe weapon · Drop to ground / stand up · Drop item · Shout a few words

### Movement
Move up to Movement rating in meters. **Dash** (action) = ×2. **Leap:** Horizontal up to half move (quarter = no roll); ACROBATICS for more. **Doors:** Unlocked = half move. **Blocking enemies:** Can't move past; must knock prone or reduce to 0 HP first. **Free Attack:** Moving away from enemy within 2m triggers EVADE roll; fail = enemy free attack (no dodge/parry). Involuntary movement never triggers this.

### Sneak Attack
SNEAKING roll required (bane if moving into melee range). Success: choose any initiative card; boon on attack; target can't dodge/parry. Subtle weapon = +1 damage die. Can't perform during active combat (except with Backstabbing).

### Melee Combat
Within **2m** (4m for Long weapons). Roll weapon skill. Success = hit. A one-handed weapon held in two hands reduces STR requirement by 3.
- **Dragon (critical):** Target needs Dragon to dodge/parry. Choose: double damage dice (before bonuses), OR free attack on another enemy, OR ignore armor (piercing only).
- **Demon:** Miss + D6 mishap (drop weapon, expose self, weapon stuck, weapon thrown, weapon damaged, hit self).
- **Prone target:** Attacker boon + extra D6 damage.
- **STR Req:** Below req = bane; below half = can't use weapon.
- **Shove:** If your STR damage bonus ≥ opponent's, shove them 2m on a hit. Monsters cannot shove or be shoved.
- **Inanimate objects:** Auto-hit; only roll for damage. Objects usually have an armor rating.

### Ranged Combat
Must be drawn (free action). Prefer >2m from target (≤2m = bane). Roll weapon skill. **Range:** Up to 2× listed range = bane. **Obscured:** Partial = bane; fully hidden = no shot. Crossbows: no damage bonus.
- **Dragon:** Double damage dice, or ignore armor (piercing only).
- **Demon:** Miss + D6 mishap (drop weapon, out of ammo, hit wrong target, break weapon, hit ally, hit self).
- **Parrying ranged:** Requires a **shield**. Works like melee parry but Dragon on parry does not grant a counterattack.

### Parrying
Declare **before** attacker rolls damage. Roll weapon skill (or any STR melee skill for a shield). Success = no damage (weapon takes hit; if damage > durability, weapon is damaged). Can't parry if turn spent or unarmed. **Piercing attacks can't damage a parrying weapon/shield.** **Dragon on parry** = free counterattack (auto-hit, no dodge/parry) — unless attacker also rolled Dragon. On success, may move self and attacker up to 2m. Monster attacks generally **can't** be parried (unless stated).

### Dodging
Declare **before** attacker rolls damage. Roll EVADE. Success = no damage; may move up to 2m. Can't dodge and parry the same attack. Can't dodge if turn spent. Monster attacks **can** be dodged (unless stated).

### Special Attacks *(declare before rolling)*
- **Find Weak Spot:** Bane on attack; hit = target treated as armorless. Piercing only.
- **Grapple:** Opposed BRAWLING. Fail = you fall. Success = both prone; enemy can't move or act. Maintaining = you can't move either. Break free = unarmed attack with boon (unblockable). Releasing enemy = free action.
- **Topple:** Opposed weapon skill vs. EVADE (toppling weapons get boon). Success = enemy prone.
- **Disarm:** Opposed weapon skill vs. weapon skill. Success = weapon lands D6m away. Bane if enemy holds it with two hands. No effect on shields or natural weapons.

### Improvised Weapons
Objects in the environment can be used as weapons. Usually single-use (consumed after). Using one is an action and can be combined with movement.

### Terrain
- **Cramped:** Bane on all melee except piercing and subtle weapons.
- **Rough:** ACROBATICS roll (not an action) each time you move; fail = fall, lose remaining movement.
- **Dimly Lit:** Bane on all ranged attacks.

### Combat on Horseback
Mount must be combat-trained. All attacks/parries use the **lower** of RIDING and weapon skill. RIDING replaces EVADE. No saddle = bane on all rolls. Enemies choose to target rider or mount; ground attacks vs. rider = bane. Mount at 0 HP = dies instantly (no death rolls).

*One suit + one helmet at a time. Cannot sleep in armor.*

**Damage Types (optional):** Leather/Studded +2 vs. bludgeoning · Chainmail +2 vs. slashing. If armor fully negates damage, weapon takes it instead (may break).

### Death
- **0 HP:** Fall down; can only try to rally.
- **Death Roll** each turn: roll vs. CON. 3 successes = recover D6 HP. 3 failures = death. Dragon = 2 successes; Demon = 2 failures.
- **Rally:** Ally within 10m uses PERSUASION (action); or self vs. WIL (bane).
- **Saving Life:** Adjacent ally rolls HEALING (action; bane without bandages). Success = stop death rolls + D6 HP.
- **Instant Death:** Single attack deals damage ≥ negative of your full HP total.
- **Additional damage at 0 HP** = automatic failed death roll.
- NPCs at 0 HP: GM decides fate. No death rolls.

## Magic

- One school as a **trained skill** (INT-based). Roll vs. school skill to cast. Fail = no effect but WP still spent.
- **Cost:** 2 WP per power level (1–3). Some spells always cost 2 WP regardless.
- **Magic tricks:** Always succeed; cost 1 WP; not subject to prepared spell limit.
- **No casting** in metal armor or with a metal weapon at hand (inventory is fine).
- **Prepared spells:** Max = INT base chance. Preparing = study grimoire for 1 shift (can happen during shift rest). Unprepared (from grimoire) = double cast time; reactions can't be cast from grimoire.
- **Power from the Body:** At ≤1 WP, roll any die (D4–D20); gain that many WP (spend immediately) but take equal damage. Not usable for healing spells.

**Requirements** (one or more): Word · Gesture · Focus · Ingredient

**Casting Time:** Action (most) · Reaction (outside turn; doesn't replace action) · Ritual (stretch or shift)

**Duration:** Instant · Round (until your next turn) · Stretch · Shift · Concentration (ends if you act, take damage, or fail WIL roll)

**Range:** Normal = hard max (unlike ranged weapons). Personal = caster only. Area of Effect = starts at caster (exempt targets with bane on roll). Sphere = all in radius except caster. Cone = width at any point = distance from source.

**Dragon:** Target must roll Dragon to resist/dodge/parry. Choose: double range or damage, OR free cast, OR no WP cost.

**Demon:** Can't push. Roll D20 magical mishap (conditions, damage, disease, random spell, curses, transformation, age change, summoning a demon).

**Learning Magic:**
- From **teacher:** 1 shift; advancement mark for school; roll INT with boon. Success = learn spell (end of session). Teacher can only raise skill by 1 before you must improve through experience first.
- From **grimoire:** Same but roll LANGUAGES without boon.
- **Magic tricks:** 1 stretch; no roll.
- **New school:** Magic Talent ability + 1 week with teacher + roll INT. Success = school at base chance.

## Monsters

### Monster Rules
Monster attacks **hit automatically** — players dodge or parry. **Ferocity** = initiative cards drawn per round (one action + movement per card). Monsters never wait. Default dodge/parry skill = 15 (flip one initiative card per use). Monster attacks generally **can't be parried** (only dodged) unless stated. Fear attacks can't be dodged or parried. Monsters immune to fear and PERSUASION (unless stated). See in darkness. Can't be disarmed or grappled (unless stated). A monster **never makes the same attack twice in a row** (re-roll → use next attack; after 6, wrap to 1).

**Natural armor** works like ordinary armor. Piercing attacks can find weak spots (optional; same rules as vs. humanoids). **Toppling** a monster: default EVADE 15; attacker gets bane if monster has 4+ legs. **Resistance:** damage of that type halved after armor (rounded up). **Immunity:** no damage from that type.

**Monster sizes** (affects blocking area and minimum passage width):

## Journeys & Wilderness

**Travel:** ~15 km/shift on foot; ~30 km/shift on horseback. Max 2 shifts/day (3rd = Exhausted; can't travel 3rd if already Exhausted). Can't march with weapons drawn.

**Pathfinding:** Appoint a pathfinder who rolls BUSHCRAFT each shift. No map = bane; spyglass = boon; difficult terrain = extra bane. Dragon = shortcut (double distance). Fail = D12 mishap (fog, blocked terrain, torn clothes, lost, dropped item, mosquitoes, sprained ankle, downpour, wasps, landslide, savage animal, quicksand).

**Random Encounters:** Once per shift (or whenever appropriate), the GM can roll for a random encounter.

**Making Camp:** Each character rolls BUSHCRAFT (bane without sleeping fur). Fail = shift doesn't count as sleep or shift rest. Tent = boon (covers multiple on one roll; small tent = 2 people, large = 6). Can't camp in dangerous locations.

**Food:** Hunting: HUNTING & FISHING to track; weapon roll/trap to kill → rations by animal (squirrel 1, crow 1, rabbit D3, fox D4, boar 2D6*, deer 2D8). *Boar attacks if hunting roll fails. Fishing: HUNTING & FISHING → D4 (rod) or D6 (net) rations. Foraging: BUSHCRAFT (bane in winter, boon in fall) → D3 rations. Raw meat/fish = disease risk (virulence 10). Raw plants = safe but 2 rations = 1 day's food. Up to 10 rations cooked in 1 shift (BUSHCRAFT roll; field/proper kitchen = boon).

## GM Tools

**Time:** 4 shifts/day — Morning → Day → Evening → Night

**Experience (end of session):** Yes to each question = one advancement mark on a skill of your choice, then roll D20 vs. skill — exceed it to gain +1 (max 18).
1. Did you participate? · 2. Explore a new location? · 3. Defeat a dangerous adversary? · 4. Overcome obstacle without force? · 5. Give in to your weakness? *(optional)*

**Advancement marks also from:** Rolling Dragon or Demon during play (tick the skill used). Teacher with skill 15+ (higher than yours): 1 shift = extra roll immediately (teacher can only raise a skill by 1; must improve through experience before getting more teacher help on that skill).

**NPC Attribute Approximation:**
STR/AGL: +D6 bonus → ~17; +D4 → ~14; none → ~10. CON = max HP (−2/Robust level). WIL = max WP if listed (−2/Focused level), else 10. INT/CHA = 10.

**Leaving an Adventure Site (D6):** 1 — Enemies follow and attack opportunely · 2 — Reinforcements (fallen ×2) · 3 — Someone else takes the treasure · 4–6 — Nothing

**NPC Creation:** Roll D4 (attitude) · D6 (kin) · D8 (motivation) · D10 (profession) · D12 (trait) · D20 (name)

**Dead PCs:** Create new character immediately. Grant extra advancement roll per session played so far. Match heroic ability count of other PCs.
